Hej, I am Sebastian Kalhöfer, a PhD candidate in Theoretical Physics at Uppsala University.

My background spans mathematics, physics, and scientific computing, with a focus on thermal quantum field theory and electron-phonon interactions in real materials.

I specialize in developing and implementing mathematical models, combining analytical theory with numerical methods and programming. Through my work, I have gained strong experience in translating complex physical systems into efficient computational frameworks and data-driven analysis.

I am particularly interested in applying these skills to quantitative problems in industry, where modelling, computation, and data analysis play a central role.
